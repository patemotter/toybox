# Tractor Ted episodes: the source of truth for the Farm stations

The dad's scene-by-scene breakdown of the modern episodes (Google Doc "Tractor Ted Episodes", 2026-10-14, checked
by him against the broadcasts). **Use it as a reference, not a checklist** (the dad: "we don't have to follow every
single piece. There's lots of tangents or extra things not worth including"). A station takes the episode's main
machine moments, in the episode's order, and skips the tangents: the children's scenes, jokes and mishaps, Les,
Tractor Ted's recaps, side trips (stables, picnics, bird-seed balls). When unsure whether a moment is main or a
tangent, leave it out or ask the dad.

**Nothing that isn't rooted in the episode** (the dad, 2026-10-14: he "will not likely enjoy the extra pieces unless
it is rooted in the episode itself"). No invented steps, machines or places; when a station is rebuilt, the extras it
has now that the episode doesn't show are questions for the dad (the grain barn in Combine Time, the silage sheet in
Glorious Grass, the slurry tanker in Mucky Muck). Kept by the dad (2026-10-15): the potato harvest in Spuds in Mud.
**Tap counts are fine as built** (the dad, 2026-10-16: one tap per bale, crate or forkful stays). **Being added**
(the dad, 2026-10-16): the forklift lifting the seed-potato bag over the planter (Spuds in Mud), the square baler
(Combine Time, Wheat), the wash plant (Munchy Crunchy).
**When in doubt, follow this guide** (the dad, 2026-10-15). Change an existing station only when it is very
obviously different from its episode and misses the episode's key pieces; small extras (the grain barn, the silage
sheet, the slurry tanker) stay. **Places look like the episode**, too: he notices
(Timberrrrr!'s sawmill was outdoors; in the episode it is inside a closed building, so now it is).
**Let's Go Racing is never built** (the dad: it "feels weird").

Build the way Timberrrrr! was rebuilt (2026-10-14, `farm/woods.js`): he does every step himself (each tap on a
machine part plays that one control's motion, the vehicles also drive by drag, the Go only points at the next
thing). The rules in AGENTS.md still apply: no Tractor Ted drawn, no Les, no milking, no numbers except at most the
episode's counting moment (Timberrrrr! counts the lorry's six wheels).

## The episodes

### Timberrrr!

- **Introduction & Problem Setup:**
  - Tractor Ted introduces the farm, Farmer Tom putting on his wellies, and Midge the dog.
  - Farmer Tom meets Les in the field to inspect damaged fence lines that need replacing.
  - Tractor Ted introduces the central inquiry: tracking where wooden fence posts come from.
- **Forestry Harvesting:**
  - An operator boards an all-terrain, multi-wheeled forestry harvester.
  - The machine drives over uneven woodland terrain on high-flotation tires to fell trees.
  - The harvester's articulated head cuts the trunk base, strips side branches through delimbing feed rollers, and cross-cuts the timber into uniform log lengths, accompanied by the harvester song highlighting its engine power and six large wheels.
- **Fencing Work on the Farm:**
  - Local children watch Farmer Tom and Les align replacement posts.
  - Les makes repeated mechanical adjustments to ensure the post is plumb before driving it into the ground.
- **Timber Hauling & Sawmill Processing:**
  - A timber lorry arrives at the sawmill, driving onto a weighbridge to record load weight.
  - A counting exercise tallies the lorry's six oversized wheels.
  - The driver operates a cab-mounted crane and hydraulic grapple to unload logs into sorted stacks, followed by a heavy wheeled loader moving logs to the processing deck.
  - Automated conveyors feed round logs through industrial blades that square off the edges and dimension the timber into finished posts.
- **Pasture Completion:**
  - Farmer Tom and Les complete the perimeter repairs back at the farm.
  - The field boundary is secured so sheep can graze safely without escaping.
  - Farmer Tom and Midge head back to the farmhouse as the workday ends.

### Munchy Crunchy

- **Introduction & Stable Visit:**
  - Tractor Ted introduces the farm, Farmer Tom, and Midge.
  - Farmer Tom visits the stable yard where Henry prepares for a pony ride.
  - Farmer Tom feeds whole carrots to the horses, while Midge inspects the feed buckets.
  - Tractor Ted poses the question of how commercial carrots are grown and harvested.
- **Field Preparation & Hand Pulling:**
  - Visiting a mature carrot crop, children pull carrots by hand for their supper, followed by a counting exercise tallying five harvested roots.
  - A front-mounted tractor topper drives down the field, slicing the leafy green tops off the carrots.
- **Mechanized Carrot Harvesting:**
  - A commercial carrot harvester lifts the roots from the soil beds.
  - The segment features the "Harvester" song detailing dirt separation and continuous lifting.
  - Harvester discharge elevators transfer carrots into an adjacent tractor trailer driving along at matched speeds, which then tips into a bulk high-sided transport trailer.
- **Yard Interlude:**
  - Back at the yard, tractor driver Les discovers his tractor cab is locked and cannot find his keys.
  - Young Alfie spots the keys and returns them to Les so work can proceed.
- **Washing, Grading & Packaging:**
  - The bulk carrot lorry unloads into an industrial wash plant.
  - Carrots travel across shaker roller tables to drop excess mud, enter a washing barrel, and get graded into large wooden storage crates.
  - A forklift stacks the crates, and carrots are fed into automatic bagging and heat-sealing machines for grocery distribution.
- **Conclusion:**
  - Farmer Tom and Midge return to the farmhouse after seeing how carrots move from field to supermarket.

### Moovie Time

- **Introduction & Ration Planning:**
  - Tractor Ted introduces the farm setup, Farmer Tom in his wellies, and Midge jumping into the farm car.
  - Farmer Tom and Les arrive at the dairy cattle housing to mix the daily ration before bringing in the herd.
- **TMR Feed Wagon Mixing:**
  - A red telehandler fitted with front bale spikes lifts wrapped silage bales, peels away protective netting, and loads them into a vertical mixer wagon.
  - The loader switches to a high-capacity bulk bucket to add loose silage and cracked corn grain to complete the total mixed ration (TMR).
  - The mixer augers blend the components into a uniform feed.
- **Barn Bedding Preparation:**
  - Children and Midge distribute clean loose straw across the open concrete yards to prepare dry bedding for the cows.
- **Herding the Dairy Cows:**
  - Due to saturated, muddy field conditions, Farmer Tom and Les walk the dairy herd down pasture tracks toward the winter barn.
  - One stubborn cow lingers behind, requiring Farmer Tom to coax it along the lane.
  - Les forgets to latch the boundary gate properly, requiring quick intervention to prevent cows from spilling back onto the track.
- **Feed Distribution & Counting:**
  - A tractor powers the loaded mixer wagon down the central feed passage, discharging feed via the side chute along the feed barrier.
  - A counting segment counts six cows feeding side-by-side along the rail.
- **Conclusion:**
  - The cattle are secured and fed; Farmer Tom and Midge walk back to the farmhouse.

### Hello Ewe!

- **Introduction & Barn Arrival:**
  - Tractor Ted introduces the farm, Farmer Tom, and Midge playing tag around the sheep pens.
  - Farmer Tom and Les prepare to manage dairy sheep milking routines and livestock husbandry.
- **Pen Clearance & Yard Scraping:**
  - A compact blue yard tractor equipped with a front loader bucket and rear rubber scraper cleans the feed passage.
  - The tractor scrapes old feed residues into consolidated piles, lifts the material, and deposits it onto the outdoor farmyard muck heap.
- **Milking Parlor Routine:**
  - The flock enters an indoor milking parlor, climbing onto an elevated milking platform.
  - Children count four sheep stepping into place, followed by an educational song detailing how sheep milk is processed into yogurt and cheese.
- **Veterinary Inspection & Foot Care:**
  - Farmer Tom flags that several sheep require hoof maintenance, prompting a veterinary check.
  - Les attempts to guide sheep into a narrow inspection race, but sheep slip past him twice before being successfully corralled.
  - The vet inspects the hoof and administers topical antiseptic blue spray to treat foot rot.
- **Straw Chopping & Bedding:**
  - While the sheep remain in the holding yard, a tractor powers a trailed straw chopper/blower down the passage, atomizing dry straw bales into a fresh bedding pack.
  - The flock re-enters the clean shed.
- **Newborn Lambs & Hand Feeding:**
  - Farmer Tom brings the children into the nursery pens to handle newly born lambs.
  - Young Alfie bottle-feeds a hungry orphan lamb, accidentally spraying milk while giggling.
- **Conclusion:**
  - Farmer Tom and Midge conclude the workday and return to the house.

### Glorious Grass

- **Introduction & Crop Assessment:**
  - Tractor Ted, Farmer Tom, and Midge assess grass maturity across the farm paddocks.
  - Tom inspects the height of the rye-grass ley to verify readiness for winter silage production.
  - Midge checks on Merlin the pony, who prefers grazing his pasture directly.
  - Children hide in tall, uncut grass, playing hide-and-seek with Les.
- **Mowing the Ley:**
  - Farmer Tom drives a tractor with front- and rear-mounted disc mowers into the field.
  - The spinning disc blades slice the crop cleanly at high speed, dropping it into flat, ventilated swaths.
- **Swathing & Raking:**
  - A secondary tractor enters with a hydraulic folding twin-rotor rotary rake.
  - The rotating tines sweep two mower swathes together into a consolidated, elevated windrow.
  - A counting exercise tallies four tractors and the self-propelled forager entering the field.
- **Forage Harvester Operation:**
  - The forage harvester picks up the continuous windrow with its front rotary pick-up reel.
  - Internal knife drums chop the grass into short lengths and blow it via a hydraulic spout into trailers running parallel alongside.
  - As one trailer hits capacity, a second tractor-trailer combo slots into place with zero downtime.
- **Silage Clamp Compaction:**
  - Trailers haul chopped grass to a concrete-walled silage clamp and tip their loads.
  - Farmer Tom operates a heavy tractor equipped with a hydraulic front buck-rake to push the forage into high layers.
  - Heavy tractor wheels roll back and forth over the pile to expel air pockets, ensuring proper anaerobic fermentation for winter silage.
- **Conclusion:**
  - Silage operations wrap up, ensuring winter feed for the cattle herd.

### Combine Time

- **Introduction & Straw Bedding:**
  - Tractor Ted introduces the farm, Farmer Tom, and Midge, then stops by the rabbit pens.
  - Children use baled straw to build a dry floor nest for three domestic rabbits.
  - Ted passes Merlin the pony, rushing to the wheat fields.
- **Crop Ripeness Inspection:**
  - Farmer Tom meets the children in the wheat field, picking ears of wheat and rubbing the husks between his palms to extract mature grain.
  - Tom demonstrates that dry, firm grain is ready for milling flour.
- **Combine Harvester Operations:**
  - Farmer Tom climbs into the high cab of a large combine harvester, lowers the cutting header, and engages the cutter bar.
  - The header cuts the stalks, feeding them into the threshing drum which separates the grain from chaff and straw.
  - The "Combine Song" details rotating cutters, continuous field passes, and the tracked undercarriage.
  - The combine swings out its discharge auger spout on the move, emptying grain into an adjacent tractor trailer.
- **Baling the Straw Residue:**
  - A tractor pulling a high-density square baler follows the combine's straw swath, compressing loose stems into rectangular bales and ejecting them along the field.
  - Midge and Tractor Ted play hide-and-seek behind the straw bales.
- **Return & Distribution:**
  - Tractor Ted explains the harvest and baling process to the children at the rabbit enclosure.
  - Ted delivers fresh straw bedding to Merlin the pony, who chews on the stalks.
- **Conclusion:**
  - Harvest operations finish and the crew heads back to the house.

### Feathered Friends

- **Introduction & Egg Decorating:**
  - Tractor Ted visits the children as they paint domestic chicken eggs green, orange, and purple.
  - Ted raises the question of commercial egg production and chicken care.
- **Poultry Barn & Vehicle Inspection:**
  - Farmer Tom and Midge visit an expansive free-range poultry unit as the birds are released from housing out onto pasture.
  - Les arrives driving an amphibious multi-wheel Argo Cat across rough field ruts.
  - Tom highlights the anatomical differences between cockerels (raised red combs) and laying hens, as well as pastured goats.
  - A comparison shows differing locomotion: goat hooves, hen toes, dog paws, and tracked machines.
- **Tracked Agricultural Tractor:**
  - The driver performs a pre-start mechanical check on a high-horsepower rubber-tracked crawler tractor, pulling the engine dipstick to verify lubrication oil levels.
  - The crawler powers up, demonstrating zero-radius skid steering without creating heavy ruts in muddy soils.
- **Bulk Hopper Feed Loading:**
  - A front-loader tractor aligns pallet forks into the base of a heavy bulk chicken feed hopper.
  - The hopper is filled from an overhead bulk feed silo, accompanied by the "Chickens" song detailing feeding routines and egg production.
- **Egg Harvesting & Picnic:**
  - Children enter nest-box areas, gathering freshly laid eggs into molded pulp packing trays.
  - Les accidentally drops an egg on the doorway step, which Midge eats.
  - Les prepares an outdoor picnic featuring hard-boiled egg sandwiches for the farm crew.
- **Conclusion:**
  - Tractor Ted returns to the farmhouse, recaps the tracked tractor and poultry operations, and concludes the day.

### Sunny Seeds

- **Introduction & Seed Ball Making:**
  - Tractor Ted and Midge visit Farmer Tom in the workshop mixing suet, lard, and sunflower seeds into winter wild-bird seed balls packed around pine cones.
  - Ted and Midge head to an adjacent farm to watch a commercial sunflower harvest.
- **Combine Header Transport:**
  - The harvesting crew prepares a tracked combine harvester.
  - The operator detaches standard grain tines from a wide sunflower header to prevent seed damage.
  - A tractor hauls the header on a specialized narrow transport trolley down rural lanes so it can fit through narrow field gates.
  - In the field, the operator docks the combine faceplate to the header and connects hydraulic lines.
- **Harvesting Dried Sunflowers:**
  - The mature sunflower crop has dropped its yellow petals, leaving heavy, desiccated heads packed with seeds.
  - The combine cuts the stalks, strips the seed heads, and threshes the seeds into internal holding bins, accompanied by the "Sunflower Song".
- **Yard Bagging Operations:**
  - High-sided trailers haul raw harvested seeds back to the hard-standing yard.
  - The trailer unloads grain into a packaging chute, filling woven bulk bags for distribution.
  - Midge sneaks a taste of the seeds before being reminded they are intended for bird feed.
- **Conclusion:**
  - Ted and Midge return to Farmer Tom, showing him the harvested seeds and finishing the wild-bird pine cone balls.

### Spuds in Mud

- **Introduction & Garden Planting:**
  - Tractor Ted and Midge find Farmer Tom in the home vegetable garden instructing children on planting seed potatoes.
  - Children dig trenches, discover earthworms that aerate the topsoil, and drop seed potatoes into the ground.
  - Midge runs off with a seed potato, and Ted heads to commercial fields to inspect mechanized planting.
- **Bed Tilling & De-stoning:**
  - A tractor pulls a heavy bed-former ridge tiller, carving deep parallel furrows in the cultivated soil.
  - A trailing de-stoner machine scoops up the ridges, passes the earth across internal web belts to screen out hard rocks, and deposits stones into an adjacent furrow to protect future root growth.
- **Mechanical Potato Planter Loading:**
  - In the farmyard, a forklift hoists heavy one-ton fabric bags of seed potatoes over a multi-row automated cup planter.
  - The operator cuts the bottom discharge chute of the bag, dumping hundreds of seed potatoes into the planter hopper.
- **Cab Ride & In-Furrow Planting:**
  - Midge climbs into the tractor cab to observe the tractor GPS guidance and hydraulic controls.
  - The planter places seed potatoes into the ridges at calibrated intervals and covers them with loose earth, accompanied by the "Planting Potatoes" song.
- **Conclusion:**
  - Ted and Midge race back to the farm garden to compare large-scale field beds with the home garden patch.

### Mucky Muck

- **Introduction & Pen Cleaning:**
  - Tractor Ted and Midge visit indoor livestock sheds where children hand-muck young lamb pens using pitchforks and wheelbarrows.
  - Farmer Tom prepares to clean out the heavy winter accumulation in the primary dairy cattle barns.
- **Cattle Relocation:**
  - Farmer Tom and Midge walk the mature cows along concrete passages into an adjacent yard, securing separation gates to allow tractor access.
- **Loader Grab Clearing:**
  - Farmer Tom operates a heavy telehandler equipped with an open-jaw hydraulic muck grab.
  - The grab bites into packed manure and straw bedding, hoisting loads high over the sides of a tractor-drawn tipping trailer.
- **Muck Heap Stacking:**
  - The trailer hauls manure out to the field margins, reverses to the field heap, and raises its hydraulic rams to dump the material.
  - The loader pushes the heap upward to minimize footprint and promote composting.
- **Muck Spreading Operation:**
  - A second tractor loads composted manure into a heavy-duty rear-discharge muck spreader.
  - The spreader drives up and down the arable fields, spinning vertical beaters to shred and broadcast the manure across the soil as organic fertilizer, set to the "Muck Spreading" song.
- **Lamb Feeding & Conclusion:**
  - Ted and Midge check on the lambs receiving fresh milk bottles in their newly bedded pens.
  - Ted recaps the nitrogen cycle of recycling barn bedding back into field fertilizer.

### Brilliant Baler

- **Introduction & Pony Yard:**
  - Tractor Ted and Midge meet children grooming ponies and feeding penned goats.
  - The children examine pony bedding made from processed Miscanthus (elephant grass).
  - Ted heads off to investigate how elephant grass is harvested and processed.
- **Forage Cutting & Drying:**
  - Ted locates the mature crop stand, noting stems reaching heights exceeding 10 feet.
  - A high-capacity forage harvester cuts the thick stalks, dropping the shredded grass directly onto the ground in swaths to dry in the wind.
- **High-Density Baling:**
  - Farmer Tom drives a tractor pulling a large square baler over the dried swathes.
  - The baler's front pick-up reel collects the crop, rams it into a compression chamber, ties knotted twine strings, and ejects dense rectangular bales.
  - The segment features the "Baler Song" and a counting segment tallying four ejecting bales.
  - A chase tractor equipped with a hydraulic squeeze grab lifts the bales and stacks them high on flatbed trailers.
- **Biomass & Chopped Bedding Processing:**
  - Tom demonstrates dual utilization: whole bales hoisted into industrial biomass combustion boilers for renewable district heating.
  - In the processing mill, a loader scoops chopped grass into bagging funnels, compressing it into sealed waterproof plastic bags for horse bedding.
- **Conclusion:**
  - Farmer Tom brings bags of elephant grass bedding back to the stables to bed down Timmy the pony.

### Mighty Mushrooms

- **Introduction & Early Morning Hauling:**
  - Tractor Ted and Midge learn that Farmer Tom departed at dawn hauling flatbed loads of wheat straw bales to supply mushroom compost producers.
  - Ted and Midge visit racing stables to observe bulk straw collection.
- **Crane Lorry Straw Loading:**
  - A heavy transport truck equipped with an integrated hydraulic crane and rotating grab lifts spent bedding straw from stable piles into high-sided haulage bins.
  - The lorry hauls the material back to the commercial composting facility.
- **Compost Fermentation Bunkers:**
  - Raw straw blends with organic poultry manure and water, moving into enclosed aerated concrete bunker sheds.
  - High internal microbial temperatures break down cellulose into rich dark substrate, set to the "Mushroom Song".
- **Mushroom Growing Tunnels & Picking:**
  - Sealed trucks deliver conditioned compost substrate to climate-controlled indoor growing houses.
  - An automated mechanical elevator lifts compost onto tiered shelf racks, topped with a moisture-retaining layer of peat.
  - Temperature and humidity controls induce button mushroom pinheads to sprout into mature caps.
  - Workers use mobile elevating platforms to hand-pick mushrooms from vertical tiers directly into distribution punnets.
- **Conclusion:**
  - Ted returns to Farmer Tom to explain how agricultural straw residue converts into commercial food crops.

### Let's Go Racing

- **Introduction & Stables Visit:**
  - Tractor Ted and Midge visit Merlin the pony to invite him to a race, but Merlin declines.
  - Ted and Midge travel to a professional thoroughbred training yard to see racehorse conditioning.
  - Children assist jockeys with "tacking up" thoroughbred racehorses with bridles and lightweight racing saddles.
- **Gallops Preparation & Harrowing:**
  - Ted inspects the artificial all-weather training surface (the gallop).
  - Farmer Tom drives a tractor pulling a specialized spring-tine harrow and leveling roller.
  - The implement breaks up compacted sand and synthetic fibers to create an even, consistent footing for running horses.
- **Spectator Transport & Training Gallops:**
  - A tractor pulling a passenger bus trailer transports the children to an elevated viewing point above the track.
  - Strings of racehorses power up the incline at full gallop past the trainers, followed by a musical feature showcasing race jockeys wearing distinct colored racing silks.
- **Wash-down & Haylage Feeding:**
  - Returning horses receive a warm-water wash down in the stable courtyard, and Farmer Tom uses the hose to wash dirt off Tractor Ted's wheels.
  - A telehandler unloads wrapped square bales of high-protein haylage into the stable feed racks.
