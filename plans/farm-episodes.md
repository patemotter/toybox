# Farm stations from the newer-series episodes

Source: a per-episode mini-game breakdown the dad passed on (2026-10-09), adapted here to the Toybox rules
(AGENTS.md: one press is always enough, short and direct, no fail states, no timing, no meters or numbers, no
sorting games, no brand names, and **never the show's characters**: no named farmer, driver, dog or pony; a generic
farmer and a farm dog are fine). Each station keeps the episode's real machines and job, which is what he knows.
Build order: the dad picks. "Spuds in Mud" and "Combine Time" are being built first.

How the breakdown's ideas map onto the system:
- **"Little problem" moments** (a jam, a dropped sheet, a stuck boot): kept as fun one-tap moments, but as
  **Surprise moments** or a single gentle step, never a failure ("Tap the big rock to pop it out!").
- **Holds, sliders, depth levers, circular swipes, timing taps:** become one tap that runs the whole motion (the
  drag or swipe stays as the richer way). No "match the frequency", no "keep it out of the red".
- **Gauges and meters** (compaction, RPM, scales): shown as the thing itself changing (the heap gets flat and
  shiny, the tank fills), never a gauge.
- **Sorting** (left/right gates, clean/muddy eggs): dropped; everything goes the one real way.
- **Milking:** dropped (the dad: he watches the animals go in, never milks).
